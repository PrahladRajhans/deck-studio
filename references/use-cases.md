## Gamma families and skins

Gamma builds many templates as **families**: one slide grammar reskinned for different moods. Lock
the family for the argument, then pick the variant for the room. Variant IDs appear to share the
parent's ID (unverified), so generate from the parent and ask for the variant's palette and imagery.
Families seen: Inflection, Account Health QBR, Shortlist, Big Bet, Beachhead, Cornerstone, Floor
Walk, Observatory, Sightline, The Quiet Deck, Verdure, Silicon Savannah, Lagoon, Tangerine (Pulse).

Gamma's 102 colour themes fall into eight tone groups (grouping is ours, from Gamma's keywords):
corporate light · corporate dark · warm editorial neutrals · luxury · tech/futuristic dark ·
fresh and calm light · vibrant and playful · retro and character. Default consulting skins:
Consultant, Pearl or Chimney Smoke (light), Slate or Founder (dark). Storytelling: Editoria,
Linen, Dune.

## Canva and Manus in one line each

- **Canva** gives the look, not the argument: titles are topic labels, charts are decorative with
  placeholder data, no exhibit numbers, so-what boxes or sources. Search by its naming language:
  colour + colour + style + use ("Blue and White Modern Business Proposal Presentation").
  Typical components to borrow: typographic cover with one accent mark, single big-stat slide,
  half-bleed photo split, Q1–Q4 roadmap cards, 2×2 audience grid.
- **Manus** (no connector; idea bank only): borrow a diagram on most slides (S-curve, chevrons,
  hub-and-spoke, impact × likelihood risk matrix) and the two-line header (action title + scope
  subtitle), but add what it lacks: one accent marking the takeaway, sources, real numbers, and
  restraint with AI-looking imagery.
